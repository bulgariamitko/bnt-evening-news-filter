# Privacy Policy – Bulgarian Evening News

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

This extension does **not** collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

- **Settings** (filter on/off, chosen BNT edition) are stored with `chrome.storage.sync`, which Chrome syncs only between your own signed-in browsers.
- **Last watched news** (video ID, title, date and playback position for each channel) is stored locally with `chrome.storage.local` on your computer only, so the popup can let you resume.
- To find the next / previous day's news, the extension requests YouTube's own channel search pages (`https://www.youtube.com/...`) directly from your browser, exactly as if you searched on YouTube yourself. No other server is contacted, and there are no analytics or tracking.

Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/bulgariamitko/bnt-evening-news-filter/issues
